## 1. SCIENTIFIC IDENTITY, NOVELTY AND OBJECTIVE

**Working title:** *U-CODE: Uncertainty-Guided Search for Reliable AI-Generated Software.*

**Core hypothesis, NOT a finding:** For independently specified and evaluated repository tasks, calibrated uncertainty and history-conditioned evidence purchase may increase independently verified full-task success relative to strong *uncertainty-removed, budget-matched search*, without manipulating coverage through abstention.

**Avoid claiming these inventions:** Bayesian experimental design, value-of-information, uncertainty, code repair, test triggering, multiobjective optimization, MCTS, abstention, Bayesian inference and finite-scenario minimax are established tools. Related systems include SWE-Search, SWE-Verifier, PATSSEL, RETRACE and SWE-Replay. Review recent work and verify citations before any new claim.

**Proposed incremental scientific mechanism:** An agent jointly decides (1) whether to spend resources on a new/repaired patch, (2) whether to purchase another test, static-analysis result or contextual observation about existing patches, and (3) whether to release/abstain. Diagnostic evidence can be correlated and its conditional likelihood may be uncertain. **Five independently enforced hard budgets** matter: tokens, test calls, GPU seconds, CPU seconds and wall seconds. Scalar prices rank feasible actions; they never override any component ceiling. Study cost, failure, risk and coverage together.

**Reference math and its limits:**
- A history-conditional binary diagnostic kernel models P(observation | latent patch acceptability, visible diagnostic history). Do not multiply tests as independent without evidence.
- One-step diagnostic net VOI = expected terminal utility after observing evidence – terminal utility now – weighted five-vector action cost.
- Finite-scenario diagnostic audit computes worst/best one-step diagnostic gain over a *frozen enumerated* set of possible likelihood models. This is not a population risk guarantee or proof the true model is included.
- The mixed-action one-step reference ranks worst-scenario diagnostic gain against **point** generation/repair forecasts.
- Newer joint-scenario reference supplies **named shared scenarios for both diagnostic and generation/repair forecasts**, rejects missing per-action scenario forecasts and chooses highest positive worst-scenario net gain or stop under five hard budgets.
- These are *one-step* and diagnostic-only look-ahead **reference modules**, not a trained, integrated, multi-patch optimal POMDP controller, nor an independently verified coding-agent result. A diagnostic with zero one-step gain may still be valuable over a longer horizon.
- Planned objective is verified full-task success **per assigned task** (abstentions count as zero success); secondary outcomes include release coverage, wrong-release risk **conditional on release**, paired delta, risk-coverage behavior and actual five-resource use. Conditional risk is undefined if no patch is released. No valid calibrated selective-risk guarantee has been proven.
