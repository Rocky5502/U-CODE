## 1. Scientific thesis, falsifiers and submission scope

**Working title:** *U-CODE: Uncertainty-Guided Search for Reliable AI-Generated Software.*

**Proposed, NOT proven contribution:** a coding agent should optimize the **marginal value of acquiring history-dependent diagnostic evidence versus generating, retrieving or repairing an alternative patch** while accounting for dependent patch/test errors and five noninterchangeable hard resource constraints—`tokens`, `test_calls`, `gpu_seconds`, `cpu_seconds` and `wall_seconds`. Decisions include generating, retrieving, static analysis, testing, repairing, releasing and abstaining.

**Closest research boundary:** search and MCTS, verification, selective acquisition, uncertainty, abstention and Bayesian VOI are existing ideas. The source-backed comparison table in `paper/overleaf/main.tex` cites **SWE-Search, GA4GC, SWE-Replay, SWE-Verifier, PATSSEL, RETRACE and Dockerless**, with neutral ✓/×/† method markers. † denotes only an offline first-party design/reference, not measured quality or superiority. Avoid false “first ever” novelty claims. Also review the original papers against current revisions before changing the table.

**Primary falsifier:** Under genuinely identical task, fixed code/model revision, seed, public proposal/tool opportunities and independently enforced five-component limits, does `ucode_conditional_bes` improve *independently verified full-task pass per assigned cell* compared with `no_uncertainty_search` without unacceptable losses in coverage, false release, resource cost or task-family generalization? Null or negative results and matched strong baselines are publishable evidence about limitations, not data to suppress.

**Six questions, preregistered in the paper:** RQ1 failure landscape/AI provenance; RQ2 pre-outcome uncertainty and calibration; RQ3 matched-budget effectiveness; RQ4 allocation of interventions; RQ5 policy and model robustness/ablation; RQ6 efficiency and generalization. Read the exact manuscript wording, not this shorthand, before revising.

## 6. Evaluation contract and metrics — exact denominators

- **Primary ITT full-task success:** released patch passes independent evaluator / all assigned task × model × seed × budget cells. Abstentions, confirmed timeouts and safety blocks remain assigned non-successes. Unknown infrastructure failures require conservative bounds.
- **Coverage:** released patches / all assigned cells, report missingness interval if infra failures leave release unknown.
- **Conditional wrong-release risk:** oracle-failed releases / independently scored releases. If there are no releases, risk is **undefined/null**, never “0%”.
- **Matched effect:** mean treatment minus control ITT success on identical task/model/seed/budget, repository/fork-family cluster uncertainty; no fake independence for repeated seeds/sibling repos.
- **Five-dimensional real cost:** independently measured tokens, test calls, GPU seconds, CPU seconds and wall seconds; reported synthetic/public receipts cannot automatically certify OS/hardware metering. Keep budget caps identical, show actual expenditures separately.
- **Attrition:** keep and distinguish abstention, timeout, safety block and infrastructure failure, with worst/best paired partial-identification bounds. These are **not** confidence intervals or proof of a risk guarantee.
- **RQ2 diagnostics:** Brier, calibration error, AUROC/AURC and cost/coverage with enough independently labeled tasks and train/validation/eval lineage separation; do not extrapolate synthetic kernel behavior.

The existing mathematical/postterminal modules enforce useful **type and accounting consistency**, not proof of statistically calibrated uncertainty, independently authored tests or authenticated execution.
