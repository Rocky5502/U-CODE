**Primary estimand:** verified full-task success **per assigned task/model/seed/budget cell** (intention-to-treat), over a strictly paired U-CODE versus no-uncertainty-search comparison. All abstentions, timeouts, safety stops and infrastructure unknowns remain visible in the denominator. Use separated five-resource realization and release/risk/coverage as complementary outcomes.

**Six machine-readable metrics in `src/ucode/research_readiness.py`:**
- `verified_full_task_success_itt`: independently verified correct release / all assigned cells.
- `release_coverage`: releases / all assigned cells.
- `wrong_release_risk`: oracle failures / independently evaluated releases; null if zero releases.
- `paired_itt_success_difference`: treatment minus control success, matched cell-by-cell.
- `five_resource_realized_cost`: five separately reported independently metered costs (once real instrumentation exists).
- `infrastructure_failure_sensitivity_bounds`: worst/best partial-identification bounds; NOT a confidence interval.

For `N` assigned cells, `s` known successful releases, `r` releases, `w` known wrong releases and `u` outcome-unknown infrastructure failures, the **prospective** reporting module uses ITT envelope `[s/N,(s+u)/N]`, coverage envelope `[r/N,(r+u)/N]` and potential wrong-release risk envelope `[w/(r+u),(w+u)/(r+u)]` when `r+u>0`. These are **sensitivity envelopes**, not confidence intervals, risk certificates or observed real experiment estimates. If `r+u=0`, wrong-release risk is **null**. Report known-release-only `w/r` separately when `r>0`. Take care not to claim a statistical guarantee from user-supplied receipts.

**Dataset-stage status:** no real metric is measurable: every observed-effect value is **N/A (null)**, not “0% success” or “0% wrong-release risk.” No accepted task, independent oracle, real inference or empirical treatment effect.
